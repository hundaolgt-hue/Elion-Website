# Reference notes

## Supplied reference: motionsites.ai "Norva Staffing Hero" (from the two hero captures)

**What it does well**
- Editorial scale: a single person per screen, a numbered index ("01"), the name set very large in a
  high-contrast serif, the role in a light sans beneath, one short paragraph, then `previous / next`.
- A giant watermark of the first name sits behind the composition at low contrast, giving depth without a shadow.
- The portrait occupies the right half, cut out on a warm paper background, with generous negative space.
- A dark bottom strip with circular thumbnails and two-line captions (project · place) anchors the frame and
  hints at more content without competing with the hero.
- Navigation is reduced to a search pill and a grid button; nothing else fights the portrait.

**What we borrow**
- The whole grammar for the founders: numbered founder, display-scale name, quiet role line, one paragraph,
  previous / next, watermark letters, portrait right, project strip at the bottom.
- The restraint: one subject per screen, very few UI elements, lots of air.

**What we beat**
- The reference is static. Our founder switch slides the text stack with a stagger and the portrait is revealed
  by a LiDAR sweep that turns points into the photograph; a drone icon travels the connecting line on entry.
- Brand fidelity: the reference mixes a serif and a sans and uses a warm paper tone; Elyon stays on Sora + Inter
  and the six blues, with Ice as the paper and Navy 900 as the strip.
- Continuity: the founders arrive on the same canvas the drone just landed on; there is no page break between
  the film and the people who fly it.

## Motion grammar applied site-wide
- Entrances `power3.out`, camera moves `power2.inOut`, counters `expo.out`; UI 0.4–1.2 s; staggers 40–80 ms.
- Text leaves by rotor wash, by dissolving into points, or by sliding under the HUD, never by a plain fade.
- Scene changes are camera moves, scale morphs (laptop), material morphs (points → mesh), the split wipe and
  the dawn-light wipe. No fade to black, no hard cut.
